# Northwind Loyalty Terms

## GOLD benefits

GOLD members receive free expedited shipping, priority support, and non-expiring store credit. Birthday month includes an extra 10% off one in-stock order.

## SILVER benefits

SILVER members receive standard free shipping on orders over $50 and store credit that expires after 12 months.

## Courtesy weather-hold exception codes

GOLD members earn two points per dollar on everyday purchases and three points on selected partner brands. Points post within forty-eight hours and appear on the monthly statement. Birthday rewards cannot be stacked with other coupons. Unused birthday discounts do not roll into the following year. Partner-brand bonuses are listed in the member portal and may change each quarter without changing the member's tier. Statement credits for returned merchandise reverse only the points from that purchase. Household sharing of points is not permitted.

GOLD members may receive a one-time courtesy weather-hold refund when exception code **NW-SHIP-EXC-04** applies to SKU **NW-HP-1001**. This courtesy is a loyalty benefit, not a shipping-policy rule. SILVER and other tiers are not eligible for this courtesy code.

Point balances are visible in the member portal. Expired SILVER points are not restored when a member later reaches GOLD. Disputes about missing points must be opened within sixty days of the purchase date.

## Points expiry

Loyalty points expire 24 months after they are earned unless the member is GOLD, in which case points do not expire.
