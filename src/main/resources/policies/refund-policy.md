# Northwind Refund Policy

## Refund request window

Customers have **30 days** from the delivery date to request a refund. The request window is measured from the carrier's recorded delivery timestamp, not from the order-placed date. After 30 days the refund request window closes and Northwind will not issue a purchase refund except where a separate written exception applies.

## Eligibility

Refunds apply to unused items in original packaging with all accessories included. Opened software, gift cards, and final-sale items are not eligible. The original payment method is credited when eligibility is met.

## Late delivery refunds

If a shipment arrives late, the customer may still request a refund within the 30-day refund request window. A late delivery by itself does not extend the window. For weather-related delays, see the shipping policy; this refund policy still governs whether money is returned versus store credit.

## Store credit

When a refund is declined for eligibility reasons, Northwind may offer store credit equal to the merchandise amount. Store credit does not expire for GOLD members and expires after 12 months for other tiers.
