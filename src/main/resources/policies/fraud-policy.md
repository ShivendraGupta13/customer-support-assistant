# Northwind Fraud Review Policy

## Chargeback review

Payments flagged for chargeback are held pending a manual review. Support must not promise a refund while a chargeback investigation is open. The fraud team records the outcome on the order.

## Multiple shipping addresses

Orders with shipping addresses that differ from the customer's usual locations, or with several destination changes in a short window, are scored as elevated risk. A high fraud score on multiple shipping addresses pauses fulfillment until the customer confirms the destination through a verified channel.

## Account takeover

Sudden contact-channel changes combined with a high-value refund request may indicate account takeover. Escalate to the fraud specialist. Do not process a refund or change the shipping address until the account holder is re-authenticated.
