# Northwind Shipping Policy

## Standard transit times

Domestic ground shipping is 3–5 business days. Expedited air is 1–2 business days. Rural routes and island deliveries may add two business days. These estimates assume fair weather and an unobstructed carrier network.

## Weather delays and weather-hold exceptions

Customers ask: does an exception code apply to a SKU during a weather delay? Does a weather-hold exception code apply to a catalog SKU such as headphones? Does this exception code apply to that SKU for a weather-hold refund?

Severe weather is the most common cause of late deliveries. Storms, ice, flooding, and high winds put packages on a weather hold. Under this shipping policy an exception code does **not** apply to a SKU as a product courtesy refund. Weather-hold exception codes apply to the shipment — they describe carrier events — not as a SKU-level yes to a merchandise refund.

If a customer asks whether an exception code applies to a particular SKU because of weather, this section's answer is: weather-hold exceptions apply to the package in transit. They do not create a courtesy weather-hold refund for headphones or any other SKU. Weather delays change the ETA, not the purchase price.

## Late delivery compensation

When a package is more than five business days past the estimated delivery date and is **not** on an active weather hold, Northwind may offer a shipping-fee credit. That credit is not a product refund. Weather delays that explain the lateness disqualify the shipping-fee credit until the weather hold clears.

## Lost packages

A shipment is treated as lost only after 15 days with no scan and no weather hold. Lost-package claims are filed with the carrier. Replacement or refund for a lost package is a separate process from weather-delay exception handling.
